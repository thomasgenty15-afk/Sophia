Yes — fibres are the part of plant foods your body doesn’t fully digest.

They help with:
- better bowel regularity
- steadier blood sugar
- longer fullness after meals
- feeding your gut bacteria

Main types:
- Soluble fibre: turns gel-like in water, helps with cholesterol and blood sugar. Found in oats, beans, lentils, apples, citrus, chia.
- Insoluble fibre: adds bulk and helps things move along. Found in whole grains, nuts, seeds, vegetable skins.

Easy rule: more whole plants, more fibre.

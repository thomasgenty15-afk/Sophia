Client: M. Alvarez

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE WE ARE

Bloodwork came back with low ferritin and vitamin D at 21 ng/mL. Energy
crashes around 3pm every day, and you told me you skip breakfast maybe
four days out of seven. We are not touching calories for now. The first
four weeks are about consistency and about getting the deficiencies
corrected. Weight is not the target this block — do not weigh yourself
more than once a week.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
DAILY NON-NEGOTIABLES

1. Protein at every meal. Aim for a palm-sized portion — 3 times a day.
   Chicken, fish, eggs, greek yogurt, tofu, lentils all count.

2. Two servings of non-starchy vegetables every day. One fist = one
   serving. Spinach, broccoli, peppers, courgette, salad, green beans.
   Frozen is fine, I would rather you eat frozen than nothing.

3. Breakfast within 90 minutes of waking. Every single day, no
   exceptions this block. This is the one that fixes the 3pm crash.

4. Water: keep a bottle on your desk. I am not going to count litres for
   you — just make sure you are drinking through the day and the urine
   stays pale.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPPLEMENTS

- Vitamin D3, 5000 IU, with breakfast (with fat — do not take it on an
  empty stomach). Dr. Reyes signed off on this dose given your 21 ng/mL.
  We retest in 8 weeks.

- Iron bisglycinate 25 mg, first thing on waking, on an empty stomach,
  WITH a glass of orange juice or a vitamin C tablet. This matters —
  vitamin C roughly doubles absorption.

- IMPORTANT: keep the iron at least 2 hours away from any dairy or
  calcium supplement. They compete. If you have your coffee with milk in
  the morning, the iron goes first, milk 2 hours later.

- Magnesium glycinate 400 mg before bed.

- Omega-3: 2 g of combined EPA+DHA per day. I do not mind whether that
  comes from a capsule or from the food — if you eat oily fish that day,
  skip the capsule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WEEKLY TARGETS

- Oily fish 3 times a week (salmon, mackerel, sardines, trout).
- Legumes 3 times a week — and I mean three different days, not three
  portions on Sunday.
- One meal out per week is planned and fine. Tell me in advance which
  day so I do not count it against you.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE ARE CUTTING

- No alcohol Monday through Friday. Weekend is your call, within reason.
- No added sugar after dinner. Fruit is fine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
MOVEMENT AND RECOVERY

- Zone 2 cardio, 3 sessions a week, 30 minutes minimum. Any day you
  like, but do not stack all three at the weekend.
- Lights out by 23:00 on weeknights.
- 10 minutes of daylight within an hour of waking — this and the
  breakfast rule work together.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT I WANT TO SEE

- Weigh once a week, Saturday morning, after the bathroom, before
  eating.
- Rate your energy 0-10 each evening.
- Photograph your breakfast every day for the first two weeks. Just
  breakfast — I do not need every meal, I need to see that it is
  happening and what it looks like.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES

Eat more fibre generally. I am not putting a number on it because you
will start weighing things and that is not what I want from you right
now — just lean towards the whole version of whatever you are already
eating.

If you travel for work, protein and breakfast are the two that survive.
Everything else can slide for those days, tell me and I will not count
